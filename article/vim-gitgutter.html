vim插件 - GitGutter

Created At：2024-05-15 23:43 Updated At：2024-05-16 22:54

vim - GitGutter插件：https://github.com/airblade/vim-gitgutter

配置项

let g:gitgutter_enabled = 1 - 自动开启gitgutter

let g:gitgutter_preview_win_floating = 1 - 使用floating/popup窗口预览git改动

美化显示效果：

let g:gitgutter_set_sign_backgrounds = 1
" vim-gitgutter used to do this by default:
highlight! link SignColumn LineNr
highlight GitGutterAdd    guifg=#009900 ctermfg=2
highlight GitGutterChange guifg=#bbbb00 ctermfg=3
highlight GitGutterDelete guifg=#ff2222 ctermfg=1

快捷键

  • 跳转到下一处改动 (change): ]c

  • 跳转到之前的改动 (change): [c

  • 暂修改存stage the hunk with <Leader>hs

  • 撤销修改undo it with <Leader>hu

  • 预览修改preview the hunk, <Leader>hp
